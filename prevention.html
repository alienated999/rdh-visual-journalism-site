After the 131st Street fire

Garage fire prevention tips

On Aug. 15, 2026, a fire on 131st Street in Coon Rapids started when batteries in the garage overheated and exploded. No injuries were reported. These tips are general homeowner practice, not a Coon Rapids Fire Department bulletin.

Batteries and charging

  • Charge on concrete, tile, or a metal shelf — not cardboard or laundry.
  • Do not leave e-bikes, tool batteries, or power stations charging overnight in an attached garage.
  • Unplug when the pack is full. Use the listed charger that belongs with the device.
  • A swollen, cracked, hissing, or wet pack does not stay in the house.

The garage

  • Keep a clear path from the house door to the overhead door.
  • Keep three feet clear in front of the service door, panel, furnace, and water heater.
  • Gas cans: approved containers, capped, away from chargers.

If a pack fails

Get people out. Call 911 from outside. Tell the dispatcher it may be a battery fire in the garage.
