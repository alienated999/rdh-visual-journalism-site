About

Robert Hildebrand

Founder and photojournalist, RDH Visual Journalism. Independent Minnesota visual journalist. Member, National Press Photographers Association. Ordained minister.

RDH Visual Journalism is an independent Minnesota-based photojournalism and documentary storytelling operation covering breaking news, public safety, fire and EMS, severe weather, community events, demonstrations, documentary work, and editorial assignments.

Services

  • Editorial and event photography
  • Portraits and business photography
  • Image licensing
  • Rapid-response news gathering
  • Videography and interviews
  • Aerial / drone coverage
  • Live streaming
